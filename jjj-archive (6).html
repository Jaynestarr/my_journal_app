Identity

I move through the world becoming.

Hi, I'm Jayne — also Ewuradjoa, also Raj, also just JJJ. Names carry worlds, and I carry mine with intention.

I write things that matter, sing when the moment asks, design when the mood strikes, and spend entirely too much time thinking about everything. A girly girl and a deep thinker. Childish in the best ways, mature where it counts. Artsy, grounded, and perpetually curious.

This is my living archive — not a portfolio that sits still, but a space that grows with me. Come back often. Something will always be different.

Roots

shapes where I'm going.

Before I was a writer or a researcher or an AI nerd — I was a baby in my mother's arms, held by someone who looked just like me. Family is the first archive. The first story told about you before you could tell it yourself.

Ewuradjoa
Anderson Aidoo.

Also Jayne · Also Raj · Also JJJ

"Ghanaian Dora the Explorer. Word lover. Certified quirky."

I started in newsrooms — JOY FM, Ghana Armed Forces Directorate of Public Relations — where I learned to tell stories clearly, deliver under pressure, and actually listen to people. Turns out listening is a skill most people underestimate.

From there I wandered (intentionally) into customer support, operations, UX research, and AI certifications. I'm tech-curious — not in a buzzword way — genuinely curious, and deeply fascinated by how people think, feel, and make decisions. Psychology, behaviour, the unspoken thing in every interaction — that's where my mind lives.

I muse too. Poems, opinions, observations, the random thought that won't leave me alone — I write those down. Some end up published. Some stay in my drafts forever. → Find my writing in The Library.

I'm building something called Margin Labs. Still taking shape. But it's mine and I'm excited about it.

I'm not sure exactly where I'm headed, but I know it lives somewhere at the intersection of people, stories, creativity and technology. Explore with me.
